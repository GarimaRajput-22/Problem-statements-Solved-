# EV Charging Analysis Dashboard (2024-2026)

## Overview
An Excel dashboard that analyses electric vehicle charging activity across 8 Indian cities. It tracks revenue, energy consumption, charging sessions, customer ratings, vehicle usage and station types.

## KPIs
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | ₹22,57,028.76 |
| Avg. Energy Consumed | 30.93 |
| Total Charging Sessions | 6,500 |
| Avg. Customer Rating | 2.99 |

## Dashboard Components
- *Vehicle Type Usage*: sessions by 2W EV, 3W EV, 4W EV and Electric Bus
- *Energy by Years*: trend of total energy consumed over time
- *Station Type Distribution*: donut chart of Fast, Standard and Super Fast chargers (about one third each)
- *Revenue by City*: average revenue per session for Pune, Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Delhi, Chennai, Bangalore and Ahmedabad

## Filters (Slicers)
- City (Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Pune)
- Membership (Member, Non-Member)
- Year / Quarter / Month

## Key Insights
1. *Balanced vehicle mix*: 2W, 4W and bus usage are nearly equal (about 205 each), with 3W EVs slightly lower.
2. *Energy demand is rising*: energy consumed grew year over year.
3. *Even station split*: Fast, Standard and Super Fast chargers each hold about 33-34%.
4. *Revenue per session is similar across cities*: about ₹340-361 per session, with Pune highest.
5. *Low customer rating*: an average of about 2.99 suggests room to improve service quality.

## Recommendations
- Investigate low ratings (wait times, charger downtime, pricing)
- Expand capacity in the highest-revenue cities
- Offer membership benefits to grow repeat usage

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Timeline)

## How to Use
1. Open the workbook in Excel.
2. Click city or membership slicers to filter the data.
3. Use the timeline to change the period.
4. All KPIs and charts update automatically.
